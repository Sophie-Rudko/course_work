Volatility & Forecast

20-day rolling volatility and next-day forecast. Metrics on a hold-out test set (last 20% of days).

Important: this page shows a risk forecast (how much the price may fluctuate tomorrow), not a buy/sell recommendation. Coursework MVP, not a trading system.

Higher predicted volatility means more uncertainty and bigger possible price swings. Lower means a calmer market.

What is "20-day rolling volatility"? Volatility is how much the daily price jumps around (the standard deviation of daily log-returns). "20-day rolling" means we recompute it every day from the last 20 trading days (about one month), so the line moves forward over time: high when recent days swing a lot, low when the market is calm. We predict tomorrow's value of this number.

Try ^GSPC or SPX for S&P 500 (assignment benchmark index).
