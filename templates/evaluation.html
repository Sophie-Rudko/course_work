Model Evaluation (Historical Backtest)

Models are evaluated on a hold-out split: the first 80% of trading days are used for training and the last 20% for testing (unseen by the model). Task: next-day volatility forecast for the S&P 500 with ML models and news sentiment.

Recommended: ^GSPC, 2024-01-01 to 2024-12-01 (ML vs baseline). Sentiment is most informative in high-volatility periods, for example 2020-02-01 to 2020-12-31 (COVID).
